SailTune plays the music on your Sailfish phone and the music on your own Jellyfin server in one app. At home or over your VPN it streams from the server; for the road, it downloads albums and playlists to the phone and plays them without any network. It opens on your music, never on a login page: the phone's music works without a server, and a server's music stays browsable when the server is out of reach.

Features
- The music on your phone, with the album art Sailfish found: artists, albums, playlists and search.
- Jellyfin servers: log in with your password or with Quick Connect, so the password need not be typed on the phone. Several servers can be added; one is active at a time.
- Downloads: albums, playlists and favorites, in their original quality. Downloads continue where they stopped after the connection or the app was gone, and pause before the phone's free space runs low. The settings show the space used and the largest albums.
- Without a network the library shows only downloaded music, with its covers; a switch shows only downloaded music at any time.
- Player on every page with 10-second skips, lock screen and cover controls, Bluetooth controls, and the exact track and position kept when the app is closed.
- Favorites and plays go to your server and come back from it. Made without a network, they are sent when the server can be reached again.
- Add tracks or a whole album to a playlist on your server, or start a new playlist.

Security and privacy
- No account, no analytics, no advertising. SailTune talks only to the servers you add.
- Your password is used once to log in and never stored. The access token lives in a file only SailTune can read; removing a server logs it out.
- Use https, or plain http only at home or through a VPN; the app says so on the login page. Certificate errors are never ignored.
- Downloads, covers and the library cache live in SailTune's private storage.

Permissions
- Audio: to play music and take the media keys.
- Music and media indexing: to find the music on the phone and its album art.
- Internet: only for the servers you add.
